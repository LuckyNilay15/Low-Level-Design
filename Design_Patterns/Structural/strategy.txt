Behavioral pattern:-
How object communicate and delegate responsibilities.

The strategy pattern:-

Navigation App(Google Maps)
Navigator class -> buildRoute() method.

Initially-> driving.
User-> walking direction(New).
Add a massive if-else block.
Bicycling(New).
Public transit(New)

if-else grow and grows.
-> voilates the open/closed principle.

# The concept and reasoning:-

Take those massive if-else -> rip out of the core class.
Put each algorithm into its own separate class("strategy").

# Reasoning:-

1) Runtime swapping:- Instantly change how an object behaves by swapping its strategy pointer.

2) Open/closed principle:- Do not touch navigator class-> Just write a new HelicopterStrategy(Thats it!!).
