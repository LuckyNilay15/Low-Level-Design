The proxy pattern:
5GB file-> load from a hard drive.
10 secs+ RAM(high) instantiate video obj.
user-> open-> play-> click.

#The concept and reasonong:
Provides a sustitute/ "placeholder" for another object.
Control(proxy) access to the original object allow us to perform something before/after the request gets through the original object.

#Reasoning:
1. Lazy initialization(virtual proxy)
2. Security (protection proxy)
3. Caching

#Visualization and High-level-design (HLD):-

               (Thinks it's talking to a real Video)
                     [ The Client App ]
                             |
                      calls play()
                             v
                 +-----------------------+
                 |     << IVideo >>      | (The Shared Interface)

                 | - realVideoPointer    |

                 | }                     |
                 +-----------------------+
                             |
                             v
                  +---------------------+
                  |   RealVideoObject   | (Massive 5GB Object)
                  +---------------------+
