The observer pattern:-

Example-> Weather Application System
# The intuition:-
WeatherStation-> measure the temperature.
-> phoneDisplay, windowDisplay, EmailAlerter.

Bad Solution 1:- (Polling)-> phone ask station every single second-> "Did temperature change?"-> CPU + Power waste.

Bad Solution 2:- (Tight coupling)-> Hardcode the display directly into the station 
{phoneDisplay->update()}.

#The concept and reasoning:-

Pub-sub mechanism-> Observer Pattern
The publisher(subject) maintain a generic list of subscribers(the observers).
-> Publisher doesn't care who the subscribers are. 
-> when publisher updates its data,it simply loops through its data and shouts the update to everyone automatically.

# Reasoning:-

1) Decoupling:- Subject doesn't know anything about the concrete observer classes.
-> update() interface.

2) Dynamic subscriptions:- We can add or remove subscribers at runtime without breaking anything.

# Visualization:-

                  [ Subject: WeatherStation ]
               Has a list: [Obs1, Obs2, Obs3...]
                               |
                        Temp changes to 75°
                               |
        (Loops through list and triggers notify() on everyone!)
              /                |                \
             v                 v                 v
     [ PhoneDisplay ]    [ WindowDisplay ]   [ EmailAlerter ]
   (Updates its screen)  (Updates its glow)  (Sends an email)
