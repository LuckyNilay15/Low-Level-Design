#Reasoning and Intuition:-

Home-theater-system-> TV + Sound Reciever + DVD Player + Smart Lights.
Execute---> Orchestrate all of these manually 
       ---> tightly coupled mess(user side).

Note: To watch a movie we have to write all those different codes every single time!.

#The concept and reasoning:-

Higher level interface(a "facade") that masks the complexity of a much larger, uglier subsystem.

Higher level interface---> HomeTheaterFacade class.
---> simple button-> watchMovie()--> heavy lifting behind the scene.

#Reasoning:-

1. Convenience-> simple entry point for a complex subsystems.
2. Decoupling-> Update TV model -> Just update the facade class , main application code-> do not change.
