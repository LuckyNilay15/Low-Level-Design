Behavioral Pattern:-

The State Design Pattern:-

Allows an object to alter its behaviour when its internal state changes.

#Intuition and analogy:-

Vending Machine:-
Behaves differently depending on its current status(state).

No coin state-----> Has coint---> dispose----> sold out 

Method 1:- If-else condition

Method 2:- delegate to a separate State object representing its current state.

# Reasoning:- The problem it solves

1. Voilates Single Responsibility Principle(SRP).
2. Voilates Open/Closed Principle(OCP).
3. High cognitive complexity
